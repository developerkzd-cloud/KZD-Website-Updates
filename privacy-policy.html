Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 May 2026  |  Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Introduction

KZD Solutions respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you visit https://kzdsolutions.co.za, contact us, request a quotation, purchase our services, register an account or interact with us in another business capacity.

We aim to process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), the applicable provisions of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA), where relevant, and other applicable South African laws.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to read this policy. Where consent is required by law, we will seek it as appropriate. Visiting our website does not, by itself, constitute consent to every form of data processing.

3. Personal Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us and which services you use.

3.1 Information You Provide

We may collect:

  • Names, surnames and business names.
  • Email addresses, telephone numbers and contact details.
  • Business addresses and billing information.
  • Account registration details and login identifiers.
  • Information submitted through contact forms, quotation requests and support tickets.
  • Information required to provide website development, hosting, domain and email services.
  • Invoices, payment references, transaction records and service history.
  • Information supplied during consultations, project discussions and customer communications.

Where necessary, we may also receive information relating to authorised representatives, domain registrants or other individuals associated with a client. Please do not send passwords, payment card security codes or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email or public contact forms.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on our website configuration and the services in use. This may include:

  • IP address and general connection information.
  • Browser type, device type and operating system.
  • Pages visited, referring websites and interaction data.
  • Dates and times of visits.
  • Server logs and security-related events.
  • Cookie identifiers and website preferences.

We use this information, where appropriate, to maintain website functionality, understand website usage, diagnose technical issues and protect our systems.

3.3 Information From Third Parties

We may receive information from payment processors, domain registrars, hosting providers, business partners and other service providers where necessary for an order, transaction, support request or contractual relationship. We will handle such information in accordance with applicable law and the purposes for which it was obtained.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries and preparing quotations.
  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Delivering website, software, hosting, domain and email services.
  • Processing orders, invoices, payments and renewals.
  • Providing technical support, troubleshooting and customer communication.
  • Managing domain registrations, DNS records and service configurations.
  • Maintaining system security, preventing fraud and investigating abuse.
  • Improving our website, services and customer experience.
  • Meeting applicable legal, accounting, tax and regulatory obligations.
  • Sending service announcements, renewal reminders and important account notifications.
  • Sending promotional communications where permitted by law and subject to applicable preferences and consent requirements.
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims where necessary.

We will process personal information only where an appropriate lawful justification exists and in a manner consistent with applicable legal requirements.

5. Lawful Processing and Data Minimisation

We aim to collect only the personal information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to perform a contract, take steps requested before entering into a contract, comply with a legal obligation, protect legitimate interests recognised by law, or fulfil another lawful basis under POPIA.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent, subject to applicable legal requirements and the consequences explained to you. Withdrawal does not automatically make earlier lawful processing invalid.

If you do not provide information necessary to fulfil a service request, we may be unable to provide the relevant service or complete the requested transaction.

6. Sharing Personal Information

We do not sell personal information as a business practice. We may disclose relevant information to trusted third parties where necessary for legitimate business operations, service delivery or legal compliance. These parties may include:

  • Hosting providers and data centre operators.
  • Domain registrars and domain registry operators.
  • Payment processors and payment gateway providers.
  • Email delivery and business communication providers.
  • Software vendors and third-party integration providers.
  • Technical contractors and support providers.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and legal representatives.
  • Government authorities, regulators or law enforcement agencies where legally required or otherwise lawfully justified.

We aim to limit disclosures to the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and to apply appropriate contractual or other safeguards where required. Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and contractual terms. Where we use a provider to process information on our behalf, we will take appropriate steps to address applicable data protection obligations.

7. Payment Information

Payments for our services may be processed through third-party payment gateways. Depending on the payment method, we may receive transaction references, payment status, amounts, dates and limited payment-related information. Payment card details may be collected and processed directly by the relevant payment provider.

We do not intend to collect or store complete payment card details or card security codes through ordinary website forms. The information actually processed will depend on the payment integration used. Payment providers may apply their own security, verification and privacy requirements.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality and security. These technologies may be used to:

  • Maintain sessions and login functionality.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Support shopping carts and account features.
  • Improve performance and diagnose errors.
  • Measure website traffic and usage, where analytics tools are enabled.
  • Support other website features disclosed at the point of use.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings or any cookie-preference tool provided on our website. Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies. The specific cookies used may change as website features and third-party integrations change.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. Depending on the service and circumstances, these measures may include access restrictions, secure connections, authentication controls, software updates, backups, monitoring and other appropriate safeguards.

No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security. Clients are responsible for protecting their own account credentials, using appropriate passwords, limiting access to authorised users and notifying us promptly of suspected unauthorised access to services we manage.

Where a security compromise affects personal information and notification is required by law, we will follow the applicable notification requirements.

10. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, contractual, operational and security requirements.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information and the relevant service. For example, invoices and transaction records may need to be retained for accounting or legal purposes, while technical logs may be retained for shorter operational or security periods.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, destroy or de-identify it, subject to lawful retention requirements, backup cycles and legitimate legal needs. Cancelling a service does not necessarily result in immediate deletion of all associated information.

11. International Transfers

Some third-party hosting, software, analytics, communication or payment providers may store or process information outside South Africa. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we will take steps to ensure that the transfer meets applicable legal requirements, including any conditions imposed by POPIA. The location of processing may depend on the provider, service configuration and infrastructure used.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate, incomplete or outdated information.
  • Request deletion or destruction of information where legally justified.
  • Object to certain processing activities where POPIA provides that right.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to direct marketing communications where applicable.
  • Request information about how your personal information is processed.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations, the rights of others or other lawful grounds. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in Section 2 and describe your request. We will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements and timeframes.

13. Direct Marketing

We may send service-related communications necessary to manage your account, orders, renewals, security notices or support requests. Where we send promotional or direct marketing communications, we will comply with applicable legal requirements, including relevant consent and objection requirements.

You may opt out of promotional email communications using the unsubscribe mechanism provided, where available, or by contacting us directly. Opting out of marketing does not prevent us from sending essential service, transactional, security or legally required communications.

14. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms, payment providers or other third-party services. We do not control the privacy practices of external websites. Visiting or using those services is subject to their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review the relevant privacy information before submitting personal information to a third party.

15. Information Processed Through Client Websites

KZD Solutions may develop, host, maintain or support websites and applications that collect personal information on behalf of our clients. Depending on the arrangement, the client may determine the purposes and means of processing, while KZD Solutions may provide technical infrastructure or process information under the client's instructions.

In these circumstances, the client's own privacy policy, notices and legal responsibilities may also apply. Individuals seeking to exercise rights regarding information collected by a client website should generally contact the relevant website operator or business. Where we are responsible for the processing in question, we will address the request in accordance with applicable law.

16. Children's Information

Our services are primarily intended for businesses and general website users. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information unnecessarily. Where a service involves children's personal information, we will handle it in accordance with the additional legal requirements that apply, including any applicable requirements relating to consent and lawful processing.

If you believe that personal information has been collected unlawfully, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, business practices or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on our website with a revised "Last updated" date. Where a change requires specific notification or consent under applicable law, we will take the appropriate steps. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
